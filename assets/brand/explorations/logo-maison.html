Pensée — the maison cut

Your Thick–Thin ¶, drawn the way a fashion house would draw it: Didone contrast (heavy stems, hairlines that nearly disappear), sharp unbracketed or ball-ended serifs, and the mark treated as one part of a system with a tracked-out wordmark and a monogram canvas. Houses keep two cuts: a delicate one for large sizes and a sturdier one for small ones.
